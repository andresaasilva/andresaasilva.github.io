About

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at UFMG, advised by Dr. Haniel Barbosa.

My research focuses on Neuro-Symbolic AI and Floating-Point Theory in Satisfiability Modulo Theories (SMT), targeting critical systems verification and safety guarantees. Prior to my graduate studies, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from UFPE.

Research

Researcher • CIn / Motorola Mobility Dec 2024 – Jan 2025
Conducted as part of an industrial R&D partnership between Motorola Mobility and the Informatics Center (CIn) of UFPE (CIn/Motorola) under the supervision of Dr. Augusto Sampaio, this research pioneered a systematic, neuro-symbolic framework for automated test-based domain model generation from natural language test cases using Generative AI, Clingo Answer Set Programming (ASP) solver for rigorous structural consistency verification, and SBERT contextualized word embeddings for semantic validation.
Researcher • INES & RoboCIn Feb 2022 – May 2023
Research on formal modeling and development of distributed systems focused on microservices and event-driven architectures for real-time multi-robot systems in association with RoboCIn, advised by Dr. Augusto Sampaio and funded by the National Institute of Science and Technology for Software Engineering (INES).
Researcher • CIn / Motorola Mobility Jan 2021 – Nov 2022
Research on path heuristics for human movement reproduction in a DENSO Robotics VS Series 6-axis articulated robot arm, focusing on capture-and-replay industrial testing at the CIn/Motorola R&D partnership, advised by Dr. Breno Miranda.
